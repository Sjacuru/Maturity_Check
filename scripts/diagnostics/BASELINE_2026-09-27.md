## Scorer behaviour, persisted run

A product with no accepted chunks produces **no block** in the user prompt, and
nothing tells the model that absence means not-evidenced. Observed effect:

| Ação | products with a block | score | uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | **2 of 9** | **3** | False |
| 14 | 3 of 5 | 3 | False |
| 40 | 3 of 4 | 3 | False |
| 2 | 1 of 4 | **0** | False |
| 20 | 1 of 5 | 0 | True |

The model's own words on Ação 31: *"Both products are adequately evidenced, so
the score for each is 3 and there is no uncertainty."* — it graded the two
blocks it received and never mentioned the other seven.

Score bands overlap almost completely against product coverage:

    score=0  n= 4  mean coverage 36%  (20–50%)
    score=1  n=17  mean coverage 52%  (25–100%)
    score=3  n= 6  mean coverage 76%  (22–100%)

30% of Ações with a missing product reported `uncertainty=False`.
2 of 27 `reasoning` fields came back in English despite a Portuguese prompt.

## Reproducibility (the target)

Four full runs reported by the user, 2026-09-27:

| class | n | Ações |
|---|---|---|
| identical in all 4 runs | 7 | 1, 2, 15, 17, 23, 25, 35 |
| varying | 26 | 3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,19,20,22,28,31,32,33,34,36,37,40,41,42,43,45 |
| empty in all 4 runs | 13 | 7,16,18,21,24,26,27,29,30,38,39,44,46 |

17 Ações produced more than one distinct score; 4 produced 0, 1 **and** 3
(19, 31, 32, 33); 14 oscillated between "no evidence" and a score.

Retrieval is deterministic (flat LanceDB scan, fixed model on CPU, FTS5, RRF
k=60 — verified index/SQLite text hashes identical), so all of this variance
originates in the gate or the scorer.

Target after the stabilisation work: **>=90% modal agreement per Ação over 10
runs**, measured by `reproducibility.py`.

## Damping available from deterministic score arithmetic

If the score were computed in code from per-product verdicts
(all=3 / some=1 / none=0), the persisted run's coverage would still be
1-flip-fragile for most Ações:

    Ações whose score changes on a SINGLE product flip : 33/46
    Ações needing >=2 simultaneous flips               : 13/46

because 19 Ações sit at coverage 0 and 4 at full coverage. The 6 single-product
Ações (3, 37, 38, 39, 44, 45) are structurally 1-flip-fragile: 0<->3 is the only
transition available. Deterministic arithmetic therefore fixes score
*correctness* and removes scorer-side variance, but does not by itself reach
90% — that needs the gate-side work.
